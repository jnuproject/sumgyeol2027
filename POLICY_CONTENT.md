# Policy fidelity requirements

The owner requires policy substance to follow the supplied planning documents.
Do not invent or change policy commitments for gameplay or narrative convenience.

- The owner's final 14-policy roster controls the displayed policy names and
  categories, including `숨결` for the council and meal-price policies. Older
  document headings/category labels do not override that explicit final list.
- Read the relevant original policy section before changing a policy explanation.
  Original documents must be obtained through the owner-authorized private channel. Never commit their links, IDs, headings or review notes to this public repository or dist. `dist/policy-sources.json` is a public policy catalog only.
  Read all relevant tabs; a blank opening template does not mean no plan exists.
- Preserve eligibility, locations, operating rules, scope, conditionality and
  implementation status. Dialogue can use natural conversational Korean and
  branching questions, but must preserve the meaning of policy claims.
- Distinguish a proposed measure from an already available service. Preserve
  requirements for university/department agreement. Do not convert examples,
  hypothetical savings or another university's prices into our promises.
- Do not fill an unknown with an invented detail, or describe a missing local
  extract as proof that the source has no plan. Flag unresolved source conflicts.
- No policy additions, substitutions or duplicate pitches beyond the final roster.
  Campus-friend greetings and directions can be fictional without policy promises.
- Game exhibit placement and dimensions are conceptual staging authorized by the
  owner, not confirmed construction or event plans. The current stadium rendering
  is not a validated rendering of the policy's three-zone allocation.
- Run roster, conversation-branch and policy-fidelity checks after changes.
  Automated wording checks cannot establish full semantic agreement; reread sources.
